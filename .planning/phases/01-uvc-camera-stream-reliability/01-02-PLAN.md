# Plan 1.2: Unify NavigationScreen with UvcCameraService and Wire Auto-Fallback

<objective>
Refactor `NavigationScreen` to consume the unified `UvcCameraService` instead of instantiating an independent `UVCCameraController`. Wire automatic camera failover (smart glasses -> phone camera) with spoken TTS announcement and haptic vibration, plus automatic recovery when reconnected.

Purpose: Guarantee that the visually impaired user is never left without camera sight or clear auditory cues when hardware cables disconnect.
Output: Integrated navigation UI with unbreakable dual-camera fallback and single-controller architecture.
</objective>

  <action>
    1. In `lib/main.dart`, instantiate a shared `UvcCameraService` in `main()` / app state and pass it to `NavigationScreen` in `onGenerateRoute`.

    4. Bind `UVCCameraView` directly to `_uvcService.controller!`.

    AVOID: Retaining duplicate controllers or calling `openUVCCamera()` from multiple places simultaneously.

<task type="auto">
  <name>Implement Automatic Fallback & Audio/Haptic Cues on Hot-Plug/Disconnect</name>
  <files>lib/ui/screens/navigation_screen.dart</files>
  <action>
    Implement hot-plug detection and failover logic in `NavigationScreen`:
    1. In the listener for `UvcCameraService`, detect when `isUvcConnected` flips from `true` to `false`:
       - Trigger a heavy haptic buzz (`HapticFeedback.heavyImpact()`).
       - Speak TTS announcement: *"Glasses disconnected. Switching to phone camera."*
       - Automatically switch `_usePhoneCamera = true` and initialize the phone camera preview without asking for touch confirmation.
    2. Detect when `isUvcConnected` flips from `false` to `true`:
       - Trigger subtle confirmation haptics (`HapticFeedback.mediumImpact()`).
       - Speak TTS announcement: *"Glasses reconnected."*
       - Switch `_usePhoneCamera = false`, attach `UVCCameraView`, and resume the wearable glasses feed.
    3. Update live frame sampling (`_captureImageSafely` and `getLatestFrameJpeg`) to pull from `_uvcService.getLatestFrame()` when glasses are active, eliminating redundant disk I/O.
    AVOID: Modal dialogs or touch prompts; the failover and recovery must be 100% automated.
  </action>
  <verify>flutter analyze lib/ui/screens/navigation_screen.dart</verify>
  <done>Automatic dual-camera failover and recovery with multimodal feedback are fully operational.</done>
</task>
