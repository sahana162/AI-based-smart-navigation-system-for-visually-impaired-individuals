# Plan 4.2: Audio Priority Preemption, Hazard Interruption & Mic Mute Window

<objective>
Harden the assistive speech delivery pipeline: ensure safety and obstacle hazard alerts instantly preempt ambient and conversational speech, silence the microphone during hazard alerts to prevent echo feedback, and deliver front-loaded concise warnings in `NavigationScreen`.

Purpose: Prevent collision warnings from being delayed behind conversational chatter, and eliminate audio feedback loops between the phone speaker and microphone.
Output: Preemptive TTS priority queue, synchronized microphone muting with acoustic grace period, and directional obstacle warnings.
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Harden TtsService Priority Preemption & Callback Lifecycle</name>
  <files>lib/core/services/tts_service.dart</files>
  <action>
    In `lib/core/services/tts_service.dart`:
    1. In `speak(String text, {TtsPriority priority = TtsPriority.ambient, bool force = false})`:
       - If `priority == TtsPriority.emergency` or `priority == TtsPriority.hazard`:
         a. If `_isSpeaking` with lower priority (e.g. `navigation` or `ambient`):
            Immediately call `await _tts.stop()` and set `_isSpeaking = false`.
         b. Clear all lower priority items from `_queue` (`_queue.removeWhere((i) => i.priority.index > priority.index)`).
    2. Add callback `Function(TtsPriority priority)? onSpeechStarting` to notify listeners (such as `VoiceSessionCoordinator`) when speech playback starts and at what priority.
    3. Ensure `onSpeechCompleted` fires reliably across platform completion, cancel, and error handlers.
    AVOID: Unhandled race conditions during rapid audio preemption.
  </action>
  <verify>flutter analyze lib/core/services/tts_service.dart</verify>
  <done>TtsService preempts low-priority audio instantly and broadcasts priority lifecycle events.</done>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Implement Microphone Mute Window and Acoustic Grace Period in VoiceSessionCoordinator</name>
  <files>lib/core/services/voice_session_coordinator.dart</files>
  <action>
    In `lib/core/services/voice_session_coordinator.dart`:
    1. Wire `ttsService.onSpeechStarting = (priority) async { ... }`:
       - If `priority == TtsPriority.emergency` || `priority == TtsPriority.hazard`:
         Immediately pause microphone if active (`await speechService.stopListening()`).
    2. In `_handleSpeechCompleted()`:
       - Maintain a 300ms acoustic grace period after TTS finishes before resuming wake-word standby or interactive listening, preventing the assistant from hearing its own speech echo.
    AVOID: Leaving microphone open while loud obstacle alarm plays.
  </action>
  <verify>flutter analyze lib/core/services/voice_session_coordinator.dart</verify>
  <done>Microphone safely mutes during hazard alerts with a 300ms echo prevention window.</done>
</task>
